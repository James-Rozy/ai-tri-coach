useWhen: Every week, same day.
examples:

Here's my week: [planned vs done, how I felt 1-10, sleep, anything that hurt, any results]. Compare what I did to the plan. Tell me what you're changing for next week and why, in two or three sentences I could repeat to a friend. Flag anything that looks like fatigue or injury risk.
