## Why this week looks like this

- **The tournament is the hard training.** Several games over three days is a lot of high-intensity work for my legs, so nothing else this week is hard. I want to show up fresh.
- **It's also a calf test.** My calves have cramped late in polo games for years, and that's the same problem that hit me at Frankfort. I'm drinking a sodium drink before every game and noting any cramp: which game, which quarter.
- **Easy cardio before polo.** I play better at night when I've done something easy that day, so Monday is an easy run and Wednesday is an easy spin instead of a swim. Polo already covers the swimming.
- **The last ride is Tuesday.** No riding from Wednesday until the 17th, and Thursday is a full rest day before an early flight.

## Last week, honestly

I trained more minutes than planned and still had a bad week. I played polo three times and skipped both long sessions. My coach's read: polo isn't just a swim, it's hard training. Three sessions on top of a max heart rate test left me sore by Saturday. After a weekend away I didn't do the long ride or the long run.

- **What changes:** polo is capped at two sessions a week during base, and never on a test day or the day before a long session.
- **Missed sessions don't get made up.** The plan just moves on.
- **The heart rate test worked.** I peaked at 182 on the final uphill sprint. That's a floor, not my true max, so I'm using 192 until a chest strap retest in December.
