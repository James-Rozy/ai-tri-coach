## Why this week looks like this

- **Every ride is outside this block.** I haven't bought a trainer, so the plan assumes Chicago roads in winter: real lights, routes I know, and if it's icy I swap the ride for an easy run or rest instead of forcing it.
- **No FTP test for now.** FTP needs power, and with no trainer and no power meter there's nothing real to measure. It waits until I have one or the other. A chest-strap max heart rate retest is planned for December 8 instead.
- **The long sessions grow slowly.** The long ride goes to 2 hours 15 and the long run to 60 minutes, on the way to 2:45 and 70 minutes by mid-December. That's the prep for the Festive 500: about 310 miles of easy riding between December 24 and 31.
- **Thanksgiving week stays light.** Monday is rest after a weekend of polo, and Thursday's ride is optional.

This was the best week of the block. Every planned ride and run happened, and my runs were finally easy: 9:18 and 9:01 a mile, after weeks of drifting into the 8s. The long ride moved to Thursday and went 37 miles at a conversational 17.6 mph.
