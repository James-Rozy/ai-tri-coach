## Why this week looks like this

- **Polo is my rhythm, so the plan is built around it.** I play Monday and Wednesday nights. Monday's polo counts as the day's session, and Wednesday gets an easy run earlier in the day.
- **The swim is optional.** Two polo nights already cover my swimming. The calf routine isn't optional. It's probably part of why I didn't cramp in Tampa.
- **The long sessions got shorter on purpose.** The long ride is 2 hours instead of 2.5, because I missed the 2-hour ride two weeks ago and this is the first long ride of the block. The long run is 55 minutes instead of 70, because my longest recent run is about 43 minutes. Jumping straight to 70 is how calves and shins complain.
