Training officially restarted on November 1. My coach built the first block from my Strava history, and the headline is that everything is easy for now. That sounds backwards after a race I lost on land, so here's the reasoning.

- **My "easy" rides weren't easy.** After the race I was averaging 18 to 20 mph on rides I called recovery. My race average was 20.3. That's close to race effort, ride after ride. Easy now means conversational, roughly 16 to 18 mph on the lakefront.
- **Hard sessions wait for real numbers.** I don't have a power meter, and my heart rate zones are guessed from my age. A max heart rate test comes first, then an FTP test on the indoor trainer in December. Intensity comes back after that.
- **The calves are the priority.** Single-leg calf raises (straight and bent knee), tibialis raises and ankle mobility, about 15 minutes, twice a week. That's the direct fix for the cramps.
- **Water polo counts as a swim.** I still play once or twice a week, and it's also the best cramp test I have.

The rule I'm trying to live by: if I miss a session, I don't make it up. If I miss two in a row, I tell my coach and it reshapes the week.
