The first thing I asked my AI coach was where the race went wrong. I gave it my splits, my Strava files and my own notes on each leg, and it came back with a breakdown I couldn't argue with: about 10 minutes on the bike, 7 on the run and 6 in transitions.

- **Transitions (about 6 minutes).** My calves cramped while I was stripping my wetsuit in T1 and I went down. The likeliest trigger is cold water (58 to 66°F) plus too hard a kick. I've cramped in cold water and late in water polo games for years.
- **Bike (about 10 minutes).** A dropped chain around mile 11, then a fade over the last quarter.
- **Run (about 7 minutes).** A stop at mile 4 for cramps and another at mile 9. Between them I was running 7:40 to 7:59 a mile, which is close to where I needed to be.

So the fix list isn't "get fitter at everything." It's a lighter kick in the wetsuit, calf strength twice a week, cold open-water swims in the spring, more bike durability, sodium before and during, and about 90 grams of carbs an hour on long rides.
